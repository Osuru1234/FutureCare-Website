OUR PROMISE

Children first.
Always.

Technology should support human care, never replace it. Every activity must respect children's dignity, safety, privacy and wellbeing.

✓ Protect children's personal information
✓ Promote safe and supervised activities
✓ Include children with disabilities
✓ Use appropriate consent and referral procedures
